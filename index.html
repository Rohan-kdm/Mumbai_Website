Business Architecture & Strategy

How Mumbai Silicon Leverages Open PCB Designs

Combining industrial open-hardware reference schematics with proprietary peripheral optimization for enterprise clients.

1. Sub-Nanosecond Clocking

We adapt high-reliability timing schematics for sub-nanosecond clock synchronization across high-speed expansion modules.

2. Multi-Layer Power Delivery

Utilizing 4-layer and 6-layer power rail decoupling stackups to guarantee zero voltage drop under heavy RISC-V workloads.

3. 90Ω / 100Ω Differential Traces

Re-using impedance-calibrated microstrip tracks to deliver crystal-clear 4K HDMI 2.0 video output and Gigabit Ethernet.

4. Rapid Turnaround R&D

By starting with pre-validated reference blocks, we reduce custom board prototyping cycles from 12 months down to 4 weeks.
